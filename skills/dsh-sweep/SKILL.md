# Sweep

A pass over a project's scaffolding — the law, the procedures, the documents as a class — run by several members at once and ended by diffs that land. It exists because scaffolding grows by accretion: a rule answers a moment, the moment passes, the rule stays, and nobody re-reads the whole of it. A sweep re-reads the whole, and its result is not a report: it is prepared changes plus the decisions the user alone owns. It is expensive, and it says so; it runs on the scaffolding, never on a task — a task has a spec, a sweep has a subject.

**Where its parts live.** The principle of the pass is the reflection's: the material is the period's tail; findings carry traces; the improvement bias is subtraction; the budget is declared before the first step; the outcome is stated as an answer to the question the pass was given. The team mechanics — the roster, the board, reports, the commit by paths — are `dsh-agent-team`'s. This file carries what a sweep owes beyond the two: the zones, the assembly and the rules its own runs paid for. The two passes answer different questions and neither runs in place of the other: the reflection ends in points and changes nothing; the sweep ends in prepared changes, one landing, one owner per file.

## Zones — fixed before the first area

The ceiling — what the pass may spend in steps or credits, and what it hands over when that is reached — is declared before the first area. With it, the sweep asks the user, in one batch with defaults:

- what may be touched — which paths are ours, which are another hand's, which are never touched;
- what the lead may land alone when the checks are green, and what waits for the user's word — a rule of the law, a published snapshot, anything contentious;
- how far the sweep goes — one subject, or the whole scaffolding;
- which theses of the user's stand in the frame, each taken as a claim under test: the sweep owes the command that confirms or refutes it, and where none exists it says so in those words instead of agreeing.

## The assembly

The shape is a floor, not a script: a subject that needs a phase this file does not name takes it and says why.

1. **Areas.** Read the scope against a named revision; divide the scaffolding into subjects, one reader each, one file each. A reader reads its subject whole — never from summaries or a previous pass's prose — and, for its own subject, writes a proposal that is not a description: what it removes, its price, the cause it answers — where the defect is produced and what other case the change catches — and what it does not do.
2. **One challenge round**, and a second only if an objection moved a proposal's ground. Critics who are not authors read from three angles: the law in force, what the change breaks, the evidence. A refusal escalates to the user and is not the truth; right numbers the critics confirmed are recorded as confirmations.
3. **Patches.** Prepared diffs, one file, one writer; `git apply --check` is the floor of the check, never its ceiling. The applied result is replayed against the intent before it lands.
4. **One landing, one hand,** where the tree is quiet; the project's cheap check runs on the result, and what was not run is named.

## What the runs paid for

Each line is written from a failure, not a preference.

- **Fix the zones in advance, and keep one owner per file for its whole life.** A write scope fixed per phase leaves an artifact nobody may correct.
- **A partial duplicate is removed partially.** Twice in one run, deleting a "restatement" carried a rule out with it: slimming removes what is written twice, not what is written once.
- **Collisions are resolved by the authors yielding,** not by the verdict: name the line, name the writers, let them settle it.
- **The lead shows its own errors.** A ledger with a corrections section — wrong numbers, invented times, a card that forgot an act — is not self-flagellation; it is the evidence that the sweep's own numbers were checked. The ledger lives in `tmp/`; what outlives the pass is what a sanctioned document carries.
- **The patch phase is not mechanical:** the findings that matter most arrive with the diffs.
- **A list of acts is a document nobody checks:** the plan's own order of acts forgot a decided act, and only the executor saw it.

What was not settled is named as not settled; a finding that could not be pinned is more useful as an open question than as a confident sentence.
